How this works: tax is calculated automatically at checkout from the zones and rates you configure below - no external tax service or API key is required.

What's a "zone"? A zone is a place you charge tax in - usually one US state, but it can also be a whole country (for a flat national rate) or, with Country left as the state's blank "—" option, every location within that country. Each zone can have one or more rates attached to it (e.g. a state rate and a separate local rate), and if a location matches more than one zone, the highest-priority zone wins.

Finding the right rate: most US states publish their current sales tax rate on their Department of Revenue (or equivalent) website - search "[state name] department of revenue sales tax rate." Some states also have county/city rates on top of the state rate; if you need to charge those too, add them as separate rates in the same zone and they'll be summed together. This app does not look up or update rates for you - revisit this page whenever a rate changes in a state you sell into.

A customer only sees tax at checkout once their state matches a zone with at least one active rate. No matching zone/rate means no tax is charged for that order - so an empty list here is the same as "not currently charging tax anywhere," which is the default for a new store.
