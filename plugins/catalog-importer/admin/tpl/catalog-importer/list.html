Import from OpenCart

Connect directly to your OpenCart store's database (read-only — nothing there is modified). Works for OpenCart 3.x and 4.x.

Import from Shopify

Upload your Shopify export CSVs (Shopify admin: Products > Export, and optionally Customers > Export). Variant pricing on single-option products (e.g. just "Size") carries over exactly; products with two option dimensions (e.g. Size and Color) keep their option/value structure but variant price differences aren't preserved — Shopify prices those per combination in a way EdgeCart's option model can't represent, so review pricing on those after import rather than trust it blindly.

Import from Etsy

Upload your Etsy shop listings CSV (Shop Manager > Settings > Options > Download Data). Etsy doesn't expose buyer email addresses to sellers, so customers can't be imported from Etsy — only categories and products.
